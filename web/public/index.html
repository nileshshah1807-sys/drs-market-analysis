    Columns

    RS
    RS rating, 1 to 99: the stock's 3, 6, 9 and 12-month performance against the Nifty 50 (the last 3 months count double). 90 and above is the best - it scores highest
    vs Nifty 3M
    3-month return minus the Nifty 50's
    Score Chg
    Change since your previous scan day ("new" = first scan)
    Traded/day
    Average daily traded value, last 20 sessions (Rs crore)
    Candle
    "1 Hour" for stocks listed less than a month ago

    Stop-loss and targets

    Stop-loss
    Just beyond the nearest support (resistance, for a sell)
    Targets
    The next resistance levels (supports, for a sell)
    No level
    A multiple of the stock's ATR is used instead
    Risk : reward
    Distance to target 2 (target 1 for a hold) over distance to the stop
